# v8.1.5 ask-only `prepared_action` experiment — 2026-05-28

Authoritative purpose: record the v8.1.5 training and evaluation run that
extended the v8.1.3pa `prepared_action` mechanism to `kernel_ask` rows
only, and document the K3 paired probe that established the causal
dependency between the lift and the presence of `prepared_action` in the
operator input.

Scope: documentation of completed work. No deployment, no adapter
install, no vLLM scale-up. The v8.1.5 adapter exists locally and in an
experiments archive; it has NOT been installed under
`/var/lib/operator-adapters/` and is NOT served by the vLLM endpoint.

## Hypothesis

If `kernel_ask` rows also receive top-level `prepared_action`, mirroring
what v8.1.3pa did for `kernel_ingest` and `kernel_write_memory`, then
`kernel_ask` becomes a copy task and exact-match should improve without
disturbing the rest of the profile.

The mechanism under test is `prepared_action.action.arguments.query` →
`action.arguments.query`. If the model emits the query verbatim from the
input, exact-match goes to 100%. The model is not asked to infer the
query from `visible_state + goal`.

## K3 paired probe — causal evidence that PA matters

The mechanism predicted that the `kernel_ask` lift is conditional on
`prepared_action` being present in the operator input at inference time.
The paired probe tests this by running the same adapter on the same 10
ask rows in two conditions: PA present, PA stripped.

Probe dir: `/tmp/operator-v815-ask-probe-20260528T134906/`
(local-stable copy: `~/ai/developents/operator-experiments/probes/k3-pa-causality-20260528/`).

Selection: 9 novel ask rows (query not in train) + 1 overlap row
(`scenario:kernel_ask:clarify-choice:0153:step:0153`, first alphabetical
step_id of the overlap bucket) = 10 rows.

Method: two Kubernetes predict jobs, identical adapter
(`6cf81a39…`), identical `--max-new-tokens 4096`, identical
batch=8, temperature=0.0, `--stop-after-json`. Datasets differ only by
the presence/absence of top-level `user.prepared_action`. Target actions
identical in both files.

### Results

| Bucket | with-PA | without-PA |
|---|---:|---:|
| total ask | 10 | 10 |
| parsed | 10 | 10 |
| `exact_match` | **10 / 10** | **2 / 10** |
| `tool_match` | 10 / 10 | 10 / 10 |
| novel exact | 9 / 9 | 2 / 9 |
| overlap exact (1 row) | 1 / 1 | 0 / 1 |
| `contract_valid` | 10 / 10 | 10 / 10 |
| `failures` | 0 | 0 |

The two without-PA wins (`evidence-query:0604`, `narrow-fact:1907`) are
rows where the target query happens to coincide with a `visible_state`
ref the model decided to copy. They are not generalization; they are
coincidences with content the model could see in the subject.

### Interpretation

1. Without PA, the v8.1.5 adapter does not generate the target query.
   It falls back to heuristic copies of refs/titles from
   `visible_state`. Performance on ask collapses to 20% on this small
   sample (small N — directionally below the v8.1.3pa baseline of
   17/32 ≈ 53%, but headline rate is noisy at N=10).
2. With PA, the adapter copies the query verbatim from
   `prepared_action.action.arguments.query`, including the 9 novel
   queries the corpus never contained.
3. The `+15` from `kernel_ask` 17/32 → 32/32 is therefore 100%
   attributable to the presence of PA in the operator input. It is not
   a generalizable improvement of query-inference ability.

## Production caveat

v8.1.5 only works in production if the subject sent to the operator
contains `prepared_action.action.arguments.query`. Today:

- The runtime previously hardcoded `prepared_action = None` while
  building `CalibrationSubject`, and the replay CLI dropped any
  `prepared_action` carried by the SFT scenario row. PR #50 fixed both
  boundaries (see `Preserve prepared_action through runtime request
  boundary`, merge commit `0f62319`).
- There is no upstream caller in production that knows the intended
  `kernel_ask` query and supplies it. No `AskPlanner`, no
  `plan_ask`, no equivalent component exists in either `operator/` or
  `rehydration-kernel/`.
- Without that upstream, v8.1.5 in production would collapse to the K3
  without-PA case, which is below the v8.1.3pa baseline. Shipping it
  without a planner would degrade ask quality.

Therefore: v8.1.5 is technically a candidate for the next manual
deployment gate, but the deployment decision must be conditioned on
either (a) building the upstream planner or (b) accepting that v8.1.5
is a batch/replay-only adapter while v8.1.3pa stays deployed.

## Decision

- Keep v8.1.3pa as the deployed candidate (SHA
  `3953786b7b18163c1e6e72b08acf5a33631f2ef6a8bb2de79c1f1fe724829156`
  under `/var/lib/operator-adapters/v8.1.3pa-candidate/`).
- Do NOT install v8.1.5 under `/var/lib/operator-adapters/`. Do NOT load
  v8.1.5 into the vLLM endpoint.
- Use v8.1.5 only in offline replay or batch jobs that supply
  `prepared_action` end-to-end (now possible after PR #50).
